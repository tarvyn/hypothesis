What matters most

Revenue and YoY growth show workload monetization; gross margin captures cloud-infrastructure efficiency; large customers track enterprise depth; and multi-product adoption is the cleanest public cross-sell signal.

What is not available

Datadog reports one operating segment. Product revenue, product COGS, and product gross margin are not disclosed, so ARR milestones cannot be converted into observed product profitability.
