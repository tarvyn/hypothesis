What matters most

NRR and GRR separate expansion from churn; large-customer cohorts track enterprise depth; and multi-product adoption is the cleanest public cross-sell signal.

What is not available

Datadog reports one operating segment. Product revenue, product COGS, and product gross margin are not disclosed, so ARR milestones cannot be converted into observed product profitability.
