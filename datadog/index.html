What changed

Spike, optimization, stabilization

  1. Q1–Q2 2021 · capacity buildCOGS grew 76% / 98% YoY versus 51% / 67% revenue growth. Filings attribute most of the gap to $18.2M / $24.7M increases in third-party hosting and software as Datadog expanded cloud capacity; GAAP gross margin fell 4 / 3 pts.
  2. Q1 2025 · mismatchLarge-customer usage spikes lifted hosting costs faster than expected; new capabilities also carried lower initial efficiency.
  3. Q2 2025 · responseEngineering cloud-efficiency projects began producing measurable savings.
  4. Q3–Q4 2025 · normalizationCOGS growth converged with revenue growth and gross-margin pressure faded.
  5. Q1 2026 · proportional scalingThe filing says revenue grew in proportion to third-party cloud infrastructure costs; the economic test now shifts to sustaining that balance.
  6. Q2 2026 · pressure returnsCOGS outgrew revenue by roughly 9 percentage points as third-party cloud infrastructure and software costs increased $64.1M year over year.

Structural read: Consumption ties telemetry growth to compute and storage costs. Hyperscaler exposure, large-customer volume discounts, and initially inefficient launches can cap gross margin near 80%; the recurring optimization cycle adds volatility, but so far has not weakened the platform moat or the model’s attractiveness.

Disclosure limit: quarterly absolute hosting cost is not reported. The $149.8M, $41.8M, and $64.1M figures are disclosed increases, not the full expense base.
