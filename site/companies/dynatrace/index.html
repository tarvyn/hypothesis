Canonical leaf rule

A leaf needs a distinct customer use case, buyer, commercial capability, or land-and-expand role. Ingest, retention, and query are billing meters—not three products.

Evidence discipline

Product pages and DPS documentation establish capability and billing. Momentum requires usage, adoption, revenue, or retention evidence scoped to that product.

Point estimate and evidence confidence are deliberately separate.

Disclosure boundary

Dynatrace reports one consolidated platform business rather than product segments. Except for logs and acquired ARR, most product momentum remains directionally assessed.
